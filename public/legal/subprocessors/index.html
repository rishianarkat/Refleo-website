REFLEO HEALTH, INC.

Subprocessors

Refleo Health, Inc. uses the following subprocessors to provide the Refleo services. Each is bound by a written agreement that requires safeguards at least as protective as those in our Business Associate Agreement, prohibits using protected health information to train artificial intelligence models, and limits retention to what is necessary to provide the service.

Subprocessor Services used Purpose Location
Amazon Web Services, Inc. Amazon Bedrock Pre-session brief generation; the model runs inside AWS-controlled infrastructure and the model provider has no access to prompts or outputs United States (us-east-1)
Amazon Transcribe Speech-to-text for voice check-ins
Amazon Cognito Sign-in and account security
Amazon SES Transactional email: reminders, verification codes, executed copies of agreements
Amazon SNS Push notification delivery
Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL Encrypted database
Amazon S3 Encrypted storage of voice recordings and executed agreements
Stripe, Inc. Subscription billing Subscription billing for clinician accounts; receives clinician name and email only, never patient information United States

Not subprocessors

Apple (push notification transport) receives device tokens and content-free notification payloads only.

Formspree, Inc. (marketing-site contact form) receives the name, email address and message a visitor chooses to submit at refleohealth.com/contact. It never handles patient or client information and has no access to the Refleo application.

Last reviewed: September 21, 2026. We review this list at least annually and whenever a new service handles protected health information. Questions: privacy@refleohealth.com.
